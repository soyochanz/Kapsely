## Style Prompt
Kapsely is a bright, social, emotional memory app. The ad should feel like a premium Instagram product launch: airy white canvas, glossy capsule objects, lively gradients, floating confetti, glass panels, and fast kinetic transitions. The visual mood is colorful, optimistic, futuristic, and intimate: "memories forever" with the energy of stories/reels.

## Colors
- Cosmic Purple: #a66eff, primary buttons, titles, main glow.
- Deep Purple: #7938ff, depth gradients and strong CTA.
- Tech Cyan: #00f2ff, accents, spark lines, secondary glow.
- Birthday Pink: #ff6fb7, emotional highlights and gift moments.
- Icy Background: #f8f9ff, clean app-like canvas.
- Ink Text: #0f0a1d, readable headline text.

## Typography
- Use Inter/Poppins-style bold geometric sans typography.
- Big punchy headlines, short Spanish copy, high contrast.

## Motion Rules
- Fast, smooth, app-native motion: scale pops, card stacks, vertical swipes, pill tabs, capsule orbiting.
- Use finite loops only. Confetti and sparkles should be deterministic and lightweight.
- Keep each scene readable for mobile: one big idea per scene.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use dark cinematic palettes.
- Do not make the ad feel like a static landing page.
- Do not overcrowd with paragraphs.
- Do not use generic stock-style imagery.
- Do not obscure text with decorative effects.
