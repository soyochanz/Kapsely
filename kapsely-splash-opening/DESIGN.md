## Style Prompt
This piece must feel like the native continuation of the Kapsely splash screen, not a separate promo. It begins on the exact same dreamy pastel canvas with the glossy capsule already visible, then gently wakes up into the product. The mood is elegant, premium, airy, and calm: subtle sparkles, frosted-glass UI, soft depth, and very refined motion. It should feel like the splash is breathing and then smoothly handing the user into the app.

## Colors
- Splash Lilac: #f3f1fe, main background wash.
- Kapsely Purple: #a66eff, primary motion accent and CTA glow.
- Deep Violet: #7c42ff, depth, focus, and active UI.
- Soft Pink: #ffb7df, emotional highlight and sparkle tint.
- Ice Blue: #bfe8ff, cool light and bottom glow.
- Ink: #17122c, final UI text.

## Typography
- Use Inter / system geometric sans.
- Bold, minimal, app-native, never editorial.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not make it feel like an ad.
- Do not introduce hard cuts or aggressive motion.
- Do not darken the scene.
- Do not cover the capsule with oversized UI.
- Do not drift away from the exact splash identity in the first second.
